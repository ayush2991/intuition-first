Intuition First is a publishing series built on a simple premise: deep and confusing concepts in physics, mathematics, and computation can be understood intuitively from the ground up—without assuming prior knowledge of advanced mathematics.

Building Intuition From the Ground Up

Technical subjects are often taught backwards: learners are handed abstract formulas, rigid definitions, and dense notation first, leaving them to guess what the math actually represents.

Whether uncovering the geometry of spacetime or unraveling information theory and entropy, each essay strips away unneeded jargon to build understanding step by step. When visual simulations clarify a physical trade-off, we provide active, hands-on controls; when conceptual clarity is what matters, we ground the math in tangible thought experiments so equations arrive naturally as descriptions of ideas you already understand.
